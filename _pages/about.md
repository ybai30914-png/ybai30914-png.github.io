I am interested in multilingual learning, language processing, and the use of computational methods to understand how people learn and use language.

I recently completed my Master of Education at Johns Hopkins University, where my work increasingly focused on multilingual learners, language and literacy development, and computational approaches to educational research.

My current research spans psycholinguistics, multilingual language and literacy, machine learning, and natural language processing. I am particularly interested in how linguistic and cultural differences shape both human language processing and the behavior of AI systems.

I am preparing to apply to PhD programs for Fall 2027.

## Research Interests

- Multilingual language and literacy
- Psycholinguistics and language processing
- AI and natural language processing for multilingual populations
- Machine learning in educational research
- Responsible and culturally aware language technologies
